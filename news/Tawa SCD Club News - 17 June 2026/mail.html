Tawa SCD Club - Special General Meeting tomorrow - and our Annual Dance lst Saturday

Hello everyone

Just a reminder that we will have a Special General Meeting (very brief!) tomorrow night, to approve the update of our club's Constitution. Please read the old and new version (attached below), so you can cast an 'informed' vote.

And now:  
 
What a great night we had at our Annual Dance on Saturday! We had six sets on the floor - three sets of our own (and former) members and three sets of dancers from other clubs, including the Roxburghs from Whanganui and the Roxburghs from Mosgiel.  

Many people heped to make it a success, and we thank them all:

  • Catherine for her great programme and faultless MCing and briefing
  • The band - Jason, Hilary and Shane - for their fabulous music
  • The people who came to help set up the hall - Catherine and Fiona with the superb stage drapes (lovingly created by Catherine a few years ago); Colin for the greenery to decorate the stage, and Denis's fairy lights; Ian and Debbie who lugged chairs and tables, and managed to get the reluctant programmes to stay stuck on the wall.
  • Denis for his welcome and closing speeches
  • Jo and her helpers - Anne, Caroline and Patricia - for organising supper, pouring coffee and tea, doing the dishes and and clearing up afterwards
  • Jo for preparing the fruit salad, and Fiona McC for the jellies 
  • Everyone for bringing their yummy supper contributions
  • Joy for her excellent photos - see below (the last two are mine) 
  • And finally, everyone who pitched in to pack up and tidy the hall. We managed to get out of there just before the 'witching' hour of 11 pm. 


Joy Tracey has posted more photos of our dance on Facebook, which you can access here. She also took some videos, but you may have to scroll down to find them.

I will send this off now (Wednesday night) as tomorrow's newsletter will have lots more in it. 

See you at club tomorrow. Remember to read the Constitution, attached below

Desiree
