Tawa SCD Club News - 29 October 2026

Hello everyone

Another good night's dancing, with three sets, but sadly some of our newer dancers were missing. Come back! We want you!  
We danced several dances that will be on the programme for our final night (programme attached). There were five dances for all, and a further two for the more experienced. Dancing was interrupted by supper and the AGM at 8pm.


Last night's dances
  • The Dancing Bees (R) -  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1535/  This was our walking warm-up dance, but in my set several people forgot we were meant to be walking, not dancing ... Devised by Roy Goldring, and very popular. This is probably one of those "must know" dances.
  • Beach Dancer (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/11587/ . Devised by a NZer, the late Eddy West from Whangarei. This is on our Final Night programme. 
  • AGM
  • Hope Little's Strathspey (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/7420/ Also devised by Roy Goldring. No info as to who Hope Little was. (Final Night)
  • Catch the Wind (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1087/ Devised by Wellington teacher Romaine Butterfield. You all know the flirty story ... Always popular everywhere. 
  • Violynne (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12567/ Devised by Johnsonville's Rod Downey for Lynne Scott, who plays the violin. (Final Night). BTW, Lynne will be playing for us on our Final Night. 
  • The Flower of Glasgow (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12941/ A lovely 3C strathspey in a 3C set, which includes a circulating Allemande, and an all round strathspey poussette. 
  • The Hazel Tree (J) -  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/2822/  It started with an Espagnole, which we practised in Strathspey time first, just to remind ourselves of the formation. Then we danced it it jig time. The dance was devised by John Drewry, so it flows beautifully, but it was just a mite challenging. We started learning this last week, but we ran out of time.

The AGM
The AGM was short and sweet, just 15 minutes. That must be a record! Here are the brief details:
  • Unusually, this was the second AGM this year, because of the change in the dates of our financial year, which now runs from 1 September to 31 August. 
  • Denis and Hilary's reports (President's and Treasurer's) were circulated before the meeting, so didn't need to be read out. Catherine read out her report. 
  • Denis briefly thanked various people for their committee work. 
  • Hilary mentioned that the club was doing OK financially, but that we needed to cover our weekly expenses (hall and tutor) mainly with the door charges, and for that we need people to turn up on club nights. We need an average attendance of 22 to break even. 
  • Elections: Denis and Hilary remain as President and Treasurer, respectively. Desiree stood down as Secretary.
  • Colin, Helen M, and Anne continue on the committee.
  • New nominations: Fiona McCutcheon for Secretary, and Louis and Caroline for committee.
  • Finally, on behalf of the club, Denis and Hilary presented Desiree (me!) with a bouquet of flowers, a framed Certificate of Appreciation, and a Prezzy card to thank her for her work as Club Secretary for the last 14 years. Joy took the photo below. 

      

Thank you from me
Thank you all for the lovely gifts, they are much appreciated. I have enjoyed being club secretary all these years. It's been good to be part of the committees and working with them towards a common goal of ensuring that our club continues to thrive. But now it's time for someone else to step into the role, and I thank Fiona for agreeing to take it on. I know she will be a brilliant secretary. At her suggestion, we will do a proper hand-over once dancing has finished for the year, so you will have to put up with me for a bit longer. 

John reckons I will suffer from withdrawal symptoms, but I think it will be great not to have to write a newsletter in the wee small hours after our Thursday classes (I am a night owl) and not to have to turn up early every week to open up the hall (though lately Denis has shared that task, thank you!). My newsletters have probably been longer than they needed to be - Denis calculated that over the years I have probably sent you 700,000 words to read (or about 1,200 A4 pages worth). Ouch! Thank you for your patience in reading them (at least, I hope you did). 

I want to acknowledge Hilary's support - her reminders of what needed to be included in the newsletters and her wise advice when I wasn't sure how to "put things" have always been much valued. Thank you. And, BTW, she has been the club's treasurer for almost as long as I have been secretary (13 years and counting!), and was a committee member before that.

Finally ...
This was to have been my final Tawa newsletter email, as I stand down as the club's secretary, but at Fiona's suggestion we will have a hand-over session after dancing has finished for the year. Have a lovely weekend. See you all next week.
