Tawa SCD Club News - 10 September 2026

Hello everyone

Wow, didn't we have a great Dessert and Dance Night! We had six sets - more than three sets of our own members, and two sets from Ngaio and a sprinkling from other clubs. It was good to see some of the Tawa members who hadn't been attending lately coming along as well. Keep that up! Stirring and lively music was provided by Mary and Rebecca on fiddles and Sharlene on keyboard. The dance went so well, that we got though all the dances on the programme faster than Catherine had planned for, but the band willingly added an extra dance. 

I have to apologise to all the people who answered the call to come early to help move the tables and chairs out of the way. I had said I'd have the door open by 6:30 - and I was late! Only by a couple of minutes, but a keen bunch of people were waiting outside a locked gate - in the rain! I am so sorry! I blame the traffic - it seems half an hour earlier than usual makes a big difference to how much traffic is about ... 

A fun evening like this takes a lot of people's input, and our thanks must go to:
  • All the keen people who helped to shift furniture, both before and after the dance
  • Catherine for a great programme, and her patient teaching/briefing
  • Mary, Sharlene and Rebecca for their wonderful music
  • Melva for closing Ngaio's club night so their members could dance with us
  • All the visitors for coming along on a wet night to join in the fun
  • All the Tawa members who brought along their delicious dessert contributions 
  • Anne, Caroline and Karen for setting out the food, serving the tea and coffee, and for clearing up afterwards
  • John for helping with washing up and taking photos


Tonight's dances
  1. Scotch Mixer (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14809/ This was our walking warm-up. It got to be a bit chaotic, and it sure did mix us all up! 
  2. Come What May (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/17900/ Devised by former Island Bay and Waikanae teacher Romaine Butterfield.
  3. First Love (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/8047/
  4. The Cranberry Tart (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/9157/ This was for the more experienced dancers. We couldn''t have a Dessert Night without The Cranberry Tart, could we? 
  5. Sean Triubhas Willichan (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5982 The Gaelic title (pronounced approximately like "shorn troos WILLI-shan") means "William's Old Trousers". 
  6. St Andrew's Fair (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5802/ This dance is on the programme for the New Dancers' Celebration
  7. On the Quarterdeck (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5080/ Devised by Wellington teacher and deviser Iain Boyd. This was the extra dance that we did to fill in the spare time...
  8. The Deil Amang the Tailors (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1582/ A great dance to finish with. Many people delight in jumping in out of turn, and confusing the beginners, but it's all part of the fun! This dance is also the final one at the New Dancers' Celebration.

Upcoming events
Programmes and cribsheets that have already been included in previous emails can also be found on our website's Events calendar page, https://www.tawascd.org.nz/events.html
  • Saturday 19 September, 7:30 pm - Wellington Region Ball. The Region is celebrating 65 years and bringing back the Region Ball with a programme including dances published by the RSCDS from devisers within the Region, as well as  old and new favourites. It will be held at Scots College. It should be a great evening. Music by Jason, Hilary, Shane Rempala and Terry Bradshaw. We encourage members to attend. This is an opportunity to dress in your best finery, and for men to don their kilts!  Cost: RSCDS members $25, Non-members $30. Pay at the door. There is no need to pre-register online. A new poster that stresses the potential elegance of the occasion is attached, as are the programme and crib sheet. 
  • Tuesdays 29 September and 6 October, 7:30-9:30 pm - Preparation classes for the New Dancers' Celebration. St David's Multicultural Church Hall, Elizabeth St, Petone. $5, pay at the door. Melva Waite will go over the dances on the NDC programme. The classes are for everyone who wants to polish up on these dances - not just the new dancers. 
  • Saturday 10 October, 7:30 pm - New Dancers' Celebration, Knox Church Hall, 574 HIgh Street, Lower Hutt. This annual Region event celebrates all the dancers in their 1st or 2nd year of dancing. A lot of fun for all - new and not-so-new.  Music by Jason, Hilary, Shane and Pip Morris. Programme and cribsheet were attached a couple of weeks ago. 
  • Saturday 17 October, 1:00-4:00 pm - Wellington Region class: John Drewry Dances, St John's Anglican Church Hall, Johnsonville. For advanced level dancers. Teacher: Damon Collin. Cost $15, register here.
