Tawa SCD Club News - 15 October 2026

Hello everyone

Another great night's dancing, with Maureen taking the class again. It was lovely to see Catherine and Fiona both coming along to dance. And some long-absent new dancers have returned - welcome back Lawrie and Michelle L. Maureen had some interesting dances for us, including one that involved partner swapping, and one with Hello-Goodbye setting  (new for some), and one with interrupted HG setting for the more experienced. New dancers went home after six dances, then two more dances for the rest of us. 


Last night's dances
  1. Crom Allt (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1426/ This was our walking warm-up dance. 
  2. Bethanket (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/15405/ Before dancing this we practised LSh reels across the dance, starting from, and finishing on, the sidelines. 
  3. Kendall's Hornpipe (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3435/ We practised the Ladies' Chain before dancing this. 
  4. The Camp of Pleasure (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/972/ An interesting strathspey which involved some partner swapping.
  5. Bill Clement MBE (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13577/ 
  6. The College Hornpipe (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1280/ We learned/practised "set to corners and partner" (the offcial name) , but more usually called "Hello-goodbye setting" 
  7. Cherrybank Gardens (S) - Devised by John Drewry. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1176/  A bit more complex - lots of things to remember. 
  8. Da Rain Dancin' (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1492/ Fast-moving, has a "3 ghillies" grading (for confident dancers). Fun to do, once you've got the hang of it. Includes an "interrupted Hello-Goodbye setting"

Last Saturday's New Dancers' Celebration
What a great night this was! There were 13 sets of dancers on the floor - i.e more than 100 people! The event was hosted by the Ngaio Club, and their tutor Melva Waite was the MC for the evening. She mentioned that 65 new dancers (i.e. who were in their 1st or 2nd year of dancing) had been sent their special invitation, and 40 of them attended the dance. Tawa did very well, with 21 of our dancers attending, including six of our newer dancers. The stage and programmes were decorated with stars, to celebrate our new "dancing stars"!  
For a full report on the dance, check out Robert Vale's story on the Johnsonville Club's website, and photos from Loralee Hyde (two of them below). Joy Tracey also put photos of the event on Facebook.
