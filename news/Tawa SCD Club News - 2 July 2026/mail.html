Tawa SCD Club News - 2 July 2026

Hello everyone

Tonight was the first of five introductory classes. We had three brand-new beginners, plus two who have been dancing at Linden since the beginning of the year and have come to 'consolidate' their skills. We also had a good turnout of existing members and regulars, so that we had three 4C sets and one 3C set (with Catherine dancing too). Thank you for helping to support the new people.  

In the first hour, which was dedicated to the beginners, we started with a warm-up and stretches in a circle, and then Catherine introduced step practice. As the beginners will be getting a separate email with the details of what they learned, I won't go into too much detail here, but Catherine covered: slip step, skip change of step, RH and LH turns, 4 hands across and back, Fig 8 across, down the middle for 3 bars, up for 3 and cast for 2.

Then she put things together into 2 dances:
  • A Jig for Jeannie (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/9102/ (note: it is called a "Reel for Jeannie", but Catherine used it as a jig)
  • Johnnie's New Reel (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3343/ (similarly, this is called "Johnnie's New Jig", but we danced it as a reel)

After this we had supper (being the first Thursday of the month) which allowed new and old to get to know one another a bit. Then the new people were able to go home - probably with their heads spinning from all they had learned today. We hope they all come back next week. And the rest of us had another three dances to complete the evening. All three are on the programme of the Levin Anniversary Dance. 
 
  1. Beach Dancer (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/11587/
  2. City of Belfast (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13847/
  3. Mairi's Wedding (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/4102/
