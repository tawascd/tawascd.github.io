Tawa SCD Club News - 19 November 2026

Hello everyone

Well, that's the 2026 Tawa SCD Club's dancing year done and dusted! And so here is my final newsletter as your secretary ...

For our Final Night, we had a fantastic evening, with six sets of dancers on the floor (and some siting out) - 30 of our own members, plus 25 visitors. It was  lovely to arrive at the hall and find that the Bowling Club had already got their Christmas decorations up, which made the place look wonderfully festive.  

The music from Rebecca, Lynne and Darryl kept us well and truly energised. We've had Rebecca and Lynne play for us before, but Darryl, playing the double bass, was new to us. Lovely to have those deep tones. Catherine's programme consisted of generally easy dances, which were all walked, with the exception of Best Set In the Hall, which was "for those that knew it well". Such a fabulous and energetic dance - it was good to have a break for supper after that, to allow us to catch our breath! 

Just before supper, Denis took to the floor to thank Catherine for all her wonderful teaching during the year. We presented her with a gift of wine and chocolates, on behalf of us all. He also thanked John for his photos, and for being on 'team Patterson', and I think he thanked me too, as standing-down secretary, but I didn't hear it, because I was in the kitchen! 

There are lots more thank yous to be handed out:
  • To Denis, for opening up in the afternoon so that the band could set up, and then opening up early for the dance, and putting the Tawa flag out the front of the hall
  • To those who came in early to help shift the tables out of the way, and line up the chairs for our guests; and to those who helped to put everything back again at the end of the night
  • To our musicians - Rebecca, Lynne and Darryl - for their great music
  • To Anne, Karen, Alison and Debbie G for organising the supper and clearing up afterwards
  • To our members for bringing along their yummy supper contributions
  • To everyone, members and guests, for their contributions to our Koha bucket. Hilary tells me it collected $349, all of which will be donated to the Wellington Food Bank. (I think she might top it up to $350 !)
  • To Hilary for organising the Koha bucket and the donation
  • To John for these photos, below.

Final Nights
It's good to hear that some members have been attending other clubs' final nights. Louis attended Linden; Denis and Alison went to Lower Hutt; Catherine, Colin and I attended Waikanae.   These are the ones I'm aware of. Others may have attended other clubs. And of course, many of us joined Ngaio for their final night last week.

There is still one more to go: Johnsonville's Tartan & Final Night, next Monday 23 Nov, at 7:30 at the Khandallah Town Hall. There will be live music from Jason, Hilary and Shane, so it should be another great night (programme and crib attached). 


Tawa Club potluck afternoon tea
I am pleased that we now have 16 people who will be attending the potluck at Hilary's home, in Aotea, on Sunday 29 Nov, 2-5pm. Please bring along a small plate of afternoon tea-type goodies to share. We're hoping for fine weather, so we can spill out into Hilary's lovely garden. If you're not on the list but would like to come, you will be very welcome, of course! Just turn up. Non-dancing partners are welcome too.

News from Jo Lockhart
I was thrilled to get a message from Jo, our former president. She seems to be enjoying life in their motorhome. She has included a couple of paragraphs for me to add to this newsletter:
"Hi all,
I've been pretty much based in Hawke's Bay since leaving Tawa at the beginning of September, enjoying the warm weather and spending time with family. We'll be here for Christmas too then heading to the South Island in February. I've missed going along to SCD!
We've had a few short trips in our motorhome, learning as we go. Currently parked up at Pourerere Beach.
All the best for Christmas and the New Year, I look forward to coming along to Tawa SCD sometime in 2027.
Take care, Jo Lockhart"

Then, in reply to my questions, she wrote this:
"We've been sleeping in our van but parked in my parents' driveway, so using the kitchen and bathroom in the house. Earlier on we did a 3-week 'loop' trip over to Taranaki then up to Waikato, catching up with friends along the way and visiting our son and his partner in Raglan.
Our first stop in the South Island is being support crew for our daughter Erin in the 2-day Coast to Coast. Then down to Mosgiel to collect Nigel's Mum for a trip to the West Coast (where she was brought up and hasn't visited for a very long time). Our plans after that are still evolving... We might do some house/pet sitting over winter. We're about to start a 2-week house sit on a farm just out of Hastings."


Carterton Annual Dance 
Last Saturday was the last of the Wellington Region clubs' Annual Dances. Eight Tawa members attended, plus Hilary who was in the band. As usual they made fabulous music. People had travelled  from other Wellington clubs and from Whanganui and Ashhurst, making a total of five or six sets. All the dances were easy and mostly well known. As Carterton had a lot of beginners this year (more than experienced members, apparently!) the first couple in each set was able to walk the dance as Elaine was briefing it. Robert Vale took the photo below of sets dancing "Demelzier". The dance finished exactly at 10pm as planned, and was followed by a sumptous supper. 



Finally ...
Yes, really finally, this is where I sign off as Tawa Club secretary. I have enjoyed being in the role for the past 14 years, but perhaps enough is enough. 

I will be handing over to Fiona at the potluck on Sunday next week (29 Nov). She will be the lucky recipient of a box of club records (the paper variety, not the musical kind) and a flash drive with a heap of files. I hope she will enjoy being the Tawa secretary as much as I have. I wish her all the best, and I know she'll do a great job. 

All that remains is to wish you all a very happy holiday season - Christmas spent in the way that you most enjoy, and good wishes for a happy and prosperous 2027. 

See you next year! (unless you're coming to the potluck!)

Cheers
Desiree
