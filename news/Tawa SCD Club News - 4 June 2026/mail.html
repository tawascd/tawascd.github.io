Tonight's dances
We got through eight dances tonight, and had supper! Next week we will do some of the remaining dances on the programme, and any from tonight that need a bit more work. 
  
Obviously all of tonight's dances are on the programme for our Annual Dance. Their descriptions are all on the attached crib sheet (below), so I won't repeat them here.
  1. The Yorkshire Rose (J) - This was our walking warm-up, and Catherine had written the description on the board for us to 'digest' before dancing it. 
  2. Flight to Melbourne (R) - A square set
  3. Athens of the North (S)  
  4. Mrs MacPherson of Inverran (R) 
  5. The Kissing Bridge (R) 
  6. The Golden Years (S)
  7. The Chequered Court (J) 
  8. John of Bon Accord (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3326/ It starts with the 1s dancing four progressive half-reels of 3 across, successively with 2L, 2M, 3L, 3M. Points to remember: 
    • The lady always finishes the half reel in the middle of the set, facing her partner;
    • The reels are R Sh, LSh, R Sh, L Sh; 
    • The supporting dancers always start the reel by going up the set; 
    • The supporting dancers are always the "tail-end charlie" of the reel; 


Our Annual Dance on Saturday 13 June (only one more week to go!)

  • Please wear your name tags. I will bring the name tag hanger 
  • Bring your own water bottle
  • Financial Tawa members will pay just $5 at the door
  • Anne and I will be minding the money table
  • Please bring two plates of finger food to contribute to supper
  • Jo, Anne and Caroline will be organising supper, and they will appreciate help from a few members to set out the supper tables and the fruit salad, and to pour cups of tea/coffee. And to help wash up afterwards. Please talk to Jo if you can help.
