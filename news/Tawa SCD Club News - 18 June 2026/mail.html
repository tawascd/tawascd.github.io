Tawa SCD Club News - 18 June 2026

Hello everyone

Tonight, as well as having a great night's dancing, we had our Special General Meeting to approve the club's updated Constitution.

As our president Denis explained, the main purpose of the review is to change the financial year's dates. The club's financial year will now run from 1 September to 31 August. This means that from this year, our AGM will take place in November, before the end of the dancing year, instead of in March/April. This is to bring it into line with other clubs in the region who all have their AGMs at the end of the year. 

Two small changes have been suggested: 
  • In the General Meetings section, move the voting section down to before the paragraph on Proxy voting. This means all the voting rules are together and clearly apply to all General  Meetings
  • Inclusion of the following sentence in the Finance section: " All payments are to be authorised by the committee". 

Denis then moved: 
The motion is that the Tawa Scottish Country Dance Club shall adopt the new Constitution that was circulated to members on 24 May 2026, and with the two amendments regarding voting and the authorisation of payments. 

The motion was carried by unanimous vote.

Next week's club night  ...
... will be 25 June, exactly six months to go before Christmas! (Aargh!) And that seems like a good excuse for a Midwinter Christmas-themed night. 

To add to the atmosphere, Catherine suggests members could bring along a small Christmas item if they wish. Preferably something that stands up, that we could stand along the tables at the top. E.g. a mini tree or mantlepiece ornament. So have a dig in that box of Xmas decorations in the garage or in the spare room cupboard, and see what you can find! 

It will be fun if we dress to the theme - e.g. wearing red or green, Xmas T-shirt, Santa hat, elf striped socks, tinsel, etc. And please bring a small plate for supper, as we have invited members of other clubs to join us. I am told there may be Christmas cake! Of course Catherine will present a programme of suitably Chrismas-themed dances
