Tawa SCD Club News - 30 July 2026

Hello everyone

Attendance was a bit sparse last night, with just 20 dancers. I guess that could be blamed on the fact that the weather seemed to have packed up again, after a few lovely, but very cold, days. 

Next week, being the first Thursday of the month, it will be a Tartan Night, so we'll have supper (cuppa & biscuits). Hopefully we will get everyone coming along. Wear some tartan if you can (but don't stress if you can't). 

Our beginners have been doing very well, and we are hoping that they will all keep on coming along to club, even though this was the last of the five 'introductory' classes. The new moves Catherine taught tonight were: dancing backwards in strathspey, dancing a circle in strathspey, and Rights and Lefts both in strathspey and quick time. 

Last night's dances
The first four dances were danced in the first hour, with the beginners; the last three were for the rest of us.

  1. Lady Catherine Bruce's Reel (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3594/
  2. The Auld Grey Cat (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/295/ Catherine modified this slightly, by replacing the reels in bars 9-16 with a Fig of  8 on the sides. 
  3. Rakes of Glasgow (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5462/ 
  4. Crabbit Shona (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13259/ 
  5. Dancing Forth (R) - Devised in 1999, but only recently published in Book 54. It includes a "swivel" before facing corners. The two halves are quite symmetrical.  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/9406/ 
  6. The Orchards of County Armagh (S) - A very gentle strathspey, with an interesting combination of half reels of 4, first up&down in the middle; then across the set; then up&down in the middle again.  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/17538/ 
  7. Mark and Susie's Jig (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/23001/ This dance has crossing reels, where each dancer crosses when they get to the top (1st place). The couple getting into the reel from 3rd place must remember to give way to the couple crossing from 1st place.

    RSCDS Fees
    RSCDS fees are due by 21 August. Hilary will email current members about this. Membership is not compulsory, but we would strongly recommend people join. If you would like to become a member, please contact Hilary . 

    Most of Tawa's current members will know that by joining the RSCDS, you are supporting the SCD community through teacher training, dance books and music CDs, and major SCD events, like Summer Schools and region classes. You get a range of benefits, including $200 off your registration fee for this year's RSCDS Summer School in Auckland

    Here are the 2025-26 RSCDS membership fees:
    Adult Joint: $106 per person
    Adult Joint Email: $98 per person
    Adult Single: $127
    Adult Single Email: $111
    Young Adult (18-24): $92
    Young Adult (18-24) Email: $76

    "Email" means you will receive the NZ Branch newsletter (Kiwi News) by email rather than as a physical copy. "Joint" is for a shared membership where two people live at the same address.

    Summer School - 28 December 2026 to 5 January 2027
    Attending Summer School is one of the great SCD experiences. Not only do you get to dance a lot, and learn heaps, but you get to meet dancers from all over NZ and quite a few from overseas. This year it will be in Auckland. If you are thinking of going, now is the time to register, as the deadline for the "early bird" registration is 31 August. After this, there will be a $100 surcharge. 

    If you think it's an expensive lark, the Branch offers several scholarships for people with fewer than 5 years, or 6 or more years' dancing experience, young adults (aged 18-24), JAMs (Junior Associate Members), musicians, and teacher prep. Details can be found on the Branch website here. You have to be a member of the RSCDS to qualify, and applications must be submitted by 30 September.
