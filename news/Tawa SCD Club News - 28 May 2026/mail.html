Tonight's dances
All except the last dance are on our Annual Dance programme. 
  1. Dame Margaret's Jig (J) - This was our walking warm-up dance. Catherine wrote the description up on the board for us to read and absorb, before dancing it. 
  2. Orpington Caledonians (R) - We did this last week. We again quickly practised the Ladies' Chain before dancing it. http://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16523/
  3. Miss Eleanor (S) - A lovely strathspey with a 3C Bourrel in it, which we practised beforehand.  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/159/
  4. On the Quarter deck (R ) - Devised by Wellingtonian Iain Boyd. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5080/ 
  5. The Gentleman (S) -https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/2437/  Finishes with an All Round Strathspey Poussette. 
  6. Pelorus Jack (J) - Devised by NZer Barry Skelton, who passed away recently.    https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/4784/
  7. John of Bon Accord (R) - Devised by Roy Goldring, for John Drewry, whose adopted hometown was Aberdeen, also known as Bon Accord. It starts with the dancing couple dancing four progressive half-reels of 3 across, successively with 2L, 2M, 3L, 3M. Points to remember: 
    • The lady always finishes the half reel in the middle of the set, facing her partner;
    • The reels are R Sh, LSh, R Sh, L Sh; 
    • The supporting dancers always start the reel by going up the set; 
    • The supporting dancers are always the "tail-end charlie" of the reel; 
  8. Farewell to Balfour Road (J ) - A fun 5C dance, where the top 2 couples dance something different from the bottom 3 couples. This is on Ngaio's Annual Dance programme, this Saturday. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/18272/
