Tawa SCD Club News - 21 May 2026

Hello everyone

My goodness, we worked hard tonight, especially Catherine! The numbers we had were difficult - 21 does not happily spread over 3C or 4C sets. But it was great to see Sharon O'M back, giving SCD another go. Thank you Duncan, for encouraging Sharon to come back. Good too, to see Laura's friend Ken back. 

Catherine is continuing to prepare us for our Annual Dance, and some of tonight's dances were a little tricky for the new-ish dancers, but with a bit of help from their partners, they generally coped OK. All except the last two dances are on the programme for the Annual Dance. After five dances, the newer dancers were able to go home, while the rest of us danced another three dances.

Tonight's dances
  1. The Yorkshire Rose (J ) - This was our walking warm-up dance. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16761/ 
  2. Orpington Caledonians (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16523/ Before dancing this, we reviewed/learned a Ladies' Chain.  
  3. The Golden Years (S) - Beware: there are four dances with the same name. We did the one devised by Nora Sutherland. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/15402/  This dance includes an 'upside down' Allemande, and two lots of Set and link, separated by 2-hand turns. We spent some time refining the Set and Link formation. 
  4. The Kissing Bridge (R) - Devised by Wellington teacher Romaine Butterfield. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14977/ It inludes the "fumbly" "cross W under the man's arm", which often causes a bit of hilarity; and it finishes with a Poussette. 
  5. Miss Eleanor (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16526/ We spent some time on practising the 3C Bourrel 
  6. Best Set in the Hall (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12426/ A great favourite, devised in NZ by Helen Greenwood. 
  7. Scott Meikle (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/10920/  Another great favourite. This is on Ngaio's Annual Dance programme. We did this twice, as it is a 4C dance in a 4C set, and is over in a flash. 
  8. Glenoe (S) - We had some time left over, and only two 3C sets left, so we finished with this 3C strathspey. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/17905/ It has two half-poussettes (which require some "foot fiddles", i.e. quick foot changes, by some), and a progressive grand chain.
