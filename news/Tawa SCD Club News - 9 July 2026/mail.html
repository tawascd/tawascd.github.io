Tawa SCD Club News - 9 July 2026

Hello everyone

My goodness, we sure are into the thick of winter now! Wasn't it cold? Full marks to those people who turned up tonight. We had 20 there, including five beginners. 

Again, the first hour tonight was aimed at the beginners. We did the same warm-up in a circle as we did last week. Then some step practise of slip step and skip change, with more emphasis on getting the detail right. We practised casting down behind one's lines; hands across; leading down for 3, back for 3, and cast one place; figs of 8, up or down;  and advance & retire. 

And Catherine put these into three dances. We did the first two last week.  
  • A Jig for Jeannie - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/9102/
  • Johnnie's New Jig (a jig, this time, not a reel!) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3343/
  • Summer Days (not the right name for a night as cold as tonight!) - I can't find a link, but as I remember it was: 1s cast behind the lines and back; fig of 8 around the 2s below; lead down for 3, back up for 3 and cast one place; Adv & ret, and turn partner RH. 

After an hour, the beginners were able to go home, and the rest of us did  three more dances, all of which are on the programme of the Levin Annual Dance. 
  1. Macdonald of Keppoch (M) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16536/ This is a medley, in a square set - i.e. it is danced twice in Strathspey time, and twice in Reel time. The special part of it is the first reel of four across the set which flows into the second reel of four up and down. Catherine was delighted that we all 'twigged' right away what was required of us. Medleys are always  fun to dance - so joyous when they switch from slow to fast.  
  2. The Luckenbooth Brooch (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3980/ This includes a "meanwhile". Do read the Extra info, to find out what a Luckenbooth Brooch is. 
  3. The Deil Amang the Tailors (R) - Also on Upper Hutt's programme. https://www.scottish-country-dancing-dictionary.com/dance-crib/deil-amang-the-tailors.html The name referes to a game of table skittles.  I'm using a different link, as this one has a picture of the Deil Amang the Tailors game.

Summer School 2026-27
It's not too early to start thinking about registering for this fabulous event, which takes place from 28 December 2026 to 5 January 2027. This year it will be held in Auckland, at the Waipuna Hotel & Conferencce Centre. As well as morning classes at all levels, there will be afternoon activities, evening dances, including Hogmanay and the President's Ball, after-parties, and a whole lot of fun. All the information you will need is on their excellent website: https://sites.google.com/view/summerschool26-27 Early bird registrations finish on 31 August. After that date it will cost you an extra $100! Get in quick! A poster is attached. 

Summer School Scholarships
The NZ Branch offers one scholarship in each of several categories. Scholarships cover the full resident Summer School fee, but exclude travel expenses. Winners are randomly selected, so every applicant within each category has an equal chance of success. https://dancescottish.org.nz/education-scholarship/ . Applications close on 30 September. 


Sad news
Frances Williamson (late of Hoon Hay SCD Club) died on 4 July, surrounded by her family. She died peacefully in Cashmere View Care Home. Frances was a long-time dancer in Christchurch, a past President of the Canterbury Region, past President of the Hoon Hay Club, and NZ Branch Editor for several years. 
An obituary will be in the upcoming July issue of Kiwi News.
