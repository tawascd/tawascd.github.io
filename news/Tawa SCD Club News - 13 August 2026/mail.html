In tonight's new dancers' section

We started with a round-the-room walking warm-up dance. Couples face each other 2 and 2, in a circle. For Lawrie and Michelle, who missed a couple of classes, the Rights and Lefts were new, so Catherine spent a bit of time explaining this formation (the details are in my email of 7 August).   

Then we reviewed last week's new formations - the Figure of 8 along the sides, and the Reel of 3 along the sides. These were "Mirror reels of 3" or also called "Reflection Reels of 3" 
  • The 1s go in and down, 2s go out and up, 3s go in and up (You will hear this description often when dances are "briefed"). 
  • In effect all three couples are covering the same path, rather like three slot cars running on the same track. 
  • To avoid collisions, the 3s (going in and up) must give way to the 1s (going in and down). 
  • https://youtu.be/OuWzbkrnyvE

We practised the Fig of 8 and the reel of 3 in the next two dances - Across Kinness Burn, and Ecclefecan Feline. The reels were Mirror reels. 

In the next dance, Reel Easy, the reels were Right Shoulder Reels (abbreviated to RSh reels). https://youtu.be/qQEowjGnDV0 
  • In this case, the 1st and 2nd couple pass each other by the right shoulder (i.e they move to their left). 
  • The 3rd couple move to their right to start the reel. 
  • The 3rd couple must give way to the 1s as they meet. 
  • The couples' reels are parallel to each other (whereas in mirror reels, they are mirror image of each other.)

Sometimes reels can be Left shoulder - when the 1s and 2s pass by the left shoulder and the 3s move to their left to begin. The 3s still have to give way to the 1s.  


Tonight's dances
The first four dances were for all, the last three were for the more experienced dancers. 
  1. Be Seeing You (J) - This was our walking warm-up dance. Round the room dance, 2 facing 2. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/431/ 
  2. Across Kinness Burn (R) - We did this dance last week. It starts with the 1s dancing figs of 8 on the sides, followed by reels of 3 on the sides. Then the 1s cross RH, cast to the bottom of the set and cross RH. Eight hands round and back. Kinness Burn is a stream in Fyfe, Scotland. There is no video. 
  3. Ecclefechan Feline (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12848/ This also included figs of 8  and mirror reels of 3 on the sides. 
  4. Reel Easy - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13827/ In this dance the reels were "Right Shoulder Reels". Unfortunately there is no video. 
  5. A Hundred Years Since (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/21332/ This dance was devised as a tribute to the centenary of the RSCDS and the Glasgow Branch. It is on the programme of the Johnsonville and Capital City Combined Annual Dance. 
  6. The St Columba's Church Strathspey (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/23888/ Devised by Rod Downey, for Johnsonville's Annual Dance. It celebrates Johnsonville Club's beginnings in St Columba's Church Hall, in Johnsonville. 
  7. Reel of the 51st Division (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5525/ An all-time favourite. Devised by Scottish POWs in Germany during WW2. Read the Extra Info for the backfround story. Often danced in  a 5C all-male set at formal dances. If you scroll to the bottom of this link https://www.scottish-country-dancing-dictionary.com/dance-crib/reel-of-the-51st-division.html , there are some fascinating videos about the history behind the 51st Division, the POWs and the dance.
