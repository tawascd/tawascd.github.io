Tawa SCD Club News - 25 June 2026

Hello everyone

What a great night we had! Despite the absolutely awful weather out there. I know some would have prefered to stay ensconced by the fire tonight! Thank goodness we have a couple of grunty heatpumps in our hall. Not surprisingly, given the weather conditions, we did not have any visitors from other clubs, apart from Chris and Andrea from Lower Hutt (they were probably strong-armed into coming by Catherine), and Linda and Ivan from Mosgiel, on their way back from visiting family in Waiouru. But we had enough of our own members to make up three sets, all up. Thank you all for turning up!

People arrived early with their Christmas decorations and tinsel. Catherine had decorated the tables at the top of the hall with our green (supper) tablecloths, festooned with cheerful Xmas bunting, and various Christmas tree, santas and tinsel spread across the tables. Tinsel was draped over chairs, noticeboard and window.


Dancers had dressed to the theme, lots of red, and green, santa hats, fancy headgear, and even a couple of Grinches


Catherine had gone to a lot of trouble to put together a programme with suitably named dances, find music that fitted not only the dances but also the theme, and found fun images to illustrate the dance names on the programme. Thank you!

And we had supper too. Thank you to everyone who brought along their goodies. Thank you to Caroline, Anne and Sharon for getting supper ready (despite Caroline still feeling a bit under the weather), and to John for doing the dishes.
