Tawa SCD Club News - 12 November 2026

Hello everyone

What a lovely night's dancing we had at Ngaio Club's Final  Night. Five sets of dancers, including 14 Tawa members, had a fabulous time, dancing to the wonderful music provided by Jason and Hilary. Melva made the evening lots of fun as usual, with her inventive ways of selecting people for a small prize of chocolates (person in the set wearing the most red, because red is the Christmas colour; person whose birhday is closest to Christmas) and forming sets (wristband with stickers to be matched). There was also a Christmas quiz, which was won by Brenda Vale. 
The dances were all firm favourites, including Ngaio's 'must-do' Trip to Bavaria. But no tuba or Bavarian hats this time!

Reminders
Next week, 19 November, will be our Final Night. Programme and crib sheet attached below. We will have music provided by Rebecca Bourne, Lynne Scott, and one more, and members of other clubs have been invited to join us. As we will be having supper, we would love our members to bring along a plate of finger food to share. We start at 7:30 - Denis wil be opening up at 7:00, and it will be good if some people could come at that time to help with moving all the tables out of the way, so as to give us plenty of room to dance.
There will be a Koha bucket by way of admission, with all proceeds to be donated to the Wellington Food Bank, so please remember to bring some cash (instead of your usual 10-trip). 

On Sunday 29 November, 2 - 5 pm, we will have a potluck afternoon tea at Hilary's home in Aotea. Last year's was very popular with 20-odd people attending. So far, I have only seven names on the list I put out last week. Please email me to let me know if you will be joining us. If we don't get at least five more names, we may have to cancel, and that would be a shame. As there usually is not much time to chat between dances on club nights, these potluck events give us an opportunity to talk, and get to know each other better.
