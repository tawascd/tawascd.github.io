Tawa SCD Club News - 24 September 2026

Hello everyone

Reminder:  The Glendarroch SCD Club Annual Dance will take place this Saturday 26 September, 2:00 pm.  Girls' College Hall, Jones Street, Whanganui. Note this is an afternoon dance. Music by Mary McDonald's Saltire Band. Programme and crib sheet attached below.   

We had a good turnout tonight, with three sets. Maureen again focused the class on Pas de Basque and "corners". Dances 2, 4, and 5 all involved corners. The newer dancwers were able to go home after number 6. The rest of us danced a further three dances. 

Last night's dances
  1. The Loch Ness Monster (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3870/ This was our walking warm-up dance in a large circle. 
  2. Granville Market (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13621/ This dance is on the New Dancers' Celebration programme. We danced it last week too. 
  3. Pas de Basque practise
  4. Reel of the 51st Division (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5525/ This is also on the NDC's programme. It involves setting (pas de Basque) and corners in a formation called "Balance in Line". This very popular dance was devised by Scottish POWs while imprisoned in Germany during WW2. Do check out the Extra Info tab on the link to find out more about its history. 
  5. Delvine Side (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1615/  
  6. Saw ye my Wee Thing (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5906/  
  7. May Yarker's Strathspey (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/10789/ This is on Glendarroch's Annual Dance programme. 
  8. Follow me Home (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/2267/ This is also on Glendarroch's programme. 
  9. Bohemian Reflections (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/17901/


New Dancers' Celebration 
Saturday, 10 October, 7:30 pm - New Dancers' Celebration, Knox Church Hall, 547 High Street, Lower Hutt. This is the Region's biggest event, as we celebrate those people who have started dancing in the past two years. For them, it will (probably) be the first time they get to dance to live music, which, as we all know, really enhances the enjoyment of dancing. The music will be provided by Jason, Hilary, Sean, and Pip Morris on drums. While the new dancers will have received special invitations (which they must bring along to show at the door to get in at a reduced charge), the rest of us are needed to come along as well, to support the new dancers. People who are not 1st or 2nd year dancers, are asked to please remember to bring along a small plate of fingerfood that does not need heating for supper. The programme and cribsheet are attached below.

To ensure full enjoyment of the occasion and for everyone to feel comfortable with all the dances, there will be two preparatory classes, on Tuesdays 29 September and 6 October, at the St David's Multicultural Church Hall, Elizabeth St, Petone. The tutor will be Melva Waite, and cost is $5 each night. These are for everyone who wants to brush up on the dances on the programme, not just the new dancers.  

Region class - John Drewry Dances - Saturday 17 October, 1-4pm
Damon Collin will be teaching this afternoon class for advanced dancers. It will be held at St John's Anglican Church hall in Johnsonville and will be from 1 pm until 4 pm.  A flyer with all the information is attached. There is a registration form on the region's website that dancers can use or just turn up and pay at the door on the day. Cost $15.


Coming up for Tawa Club
AGM - On Thursday 29 October, at around 8pm on a club night, we will be having our AGM. You will remember that, at a Special General Meeting on18 June 2026, the club changed its financial year date from 31 December to 31 August, to bring it into line with other clubs' financial year dates. Consequently the date of our AGM has been changed from March/April to October. 
Of course, we would like as many of you as possible to attend. It should not take too much time out of our dancing time. 
An AGM means the election of officers and committee members. 
At the moment, Denis is willing to continue as President, as is Hilary as Treasurer, although she has said that if anyone would love to take on this job, she would be happy step aside. However, I wish to stand down as Secretary (after 13 years). We have just three committee members (Colin, Anne, and Helen M), we should probably have four. And according to the Consitution, we should have a Vice president.
Please give some serious thought to whether you could commit to joining the committee and help in running YOUR club. It is not a difficult or time-consuming job, if everyone works together.   A nomination form is attached. If you nominate someone other than yourself, please ensure that person is willing. 

Final Night - Thursday 19 November, 7:30pm. We will invite other clubs to join us. There will be live music from Rebecca Bourne and friends, and we will have supper. 

End-of-year Potluck - Sunday 29 November, 2-5pm. After last year's success, we will again have an afternoon tea potluck. Hilary has agreed to host it again. Please mark the date in your calendar.

Region Ball
Last Saturday, the Region held its Ball at Scots College. It was a great night, lovely programme of interesting dances, and fabulous band - Jason, Hilary and Sean, with added interest from Terry Bradshaw on drums, which gave the sound extra pzazz. The hall is huge and would easily accommodate 16 sets (as it did when the Wellington Summer School was held there a couple of years ago). It was rather disappointing that attendance was very low - just four sets. There were just two of us from Tawa - Sean and myself, plus occasional Tawa attendees Joy, Diane B and Fay. And of course, Hilary up on stage with the band. The rest of you, you missed a treat! Full marks to Sean, who, despite this being just his first year dancing, joined in with most of the dances and did extremely well!
