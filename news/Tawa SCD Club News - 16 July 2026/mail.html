Tawa SCD Club News - 16 July 2026

Hello everyone

A very busy night's dancing tonight, with four dances for the first hour-and-a-bit for the beginners, plus another three for the rest of us. We had three sets, including five of the beginners, and a visitor - Sharlene Penman from Christchurch, whom many of you will know as a musician. She is in Wellington as she will be part of Mary's Saltaire Scottish Dance Band, which will be playing for the Levin Dance on Saturday.  

So here is a reminder about the Levin Annual Dance, their 70th Anniversary, this coming Saturday, at 2pm. More details below.  

Tonight's dances
We started with a walking warm-up dance, Happy to Meet, which included two new moves for the beginners - Advance and Retire, and Back-to-back. 
Summer Mondays - which we did last week
Then step practise, during which Catherine introduced Strathspey setting step and Strathspey travelling step.
And a new formation: the Allemande. 
Sandy O'er the Lea (S) -https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5889/ with a slight variation in bars 17-24 (see if you can figure out the difference was ...) Includes an Allemande in Strathspey time.
Allemande to Go (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16844/ . A chance to try an Allemande in quick time.  

After these four dances, the beginners were able to go home, and the rest of us did three more dances, all of which are on the programme of the Levin Dance on Saturday. 
The Cranberry Tart (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/9157/ The first 16 bars are a 'canon' (or the description calls it a 'fugue'), in which the lady repeats what the man does, but two bars later.  
The Water of Leith (S) - One of my favourite dances.  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/15649/ The dance starts with the 1s and the 3s. It finishes with an Espagnole.
The Firweorks Reel (R) -https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13544/ We spent quite a long while on this dance, as it ends with some confusing reels (half reels along the sides, followed by a half reels across)
