Tawa SCD Club News - 9 October 2026

Good afternoon

Our thoughts are with Catherine and Fiona, after their husband and father, Erroll, passed away suddenly, last weekend. Those attending club last night signed a condolence card for them, which was posted today. Denis has arranged for some flowers to be delivered to Catherine's home, on behalf of us all. 

In Catherine's absence, Maureen willingly stepped in to teach us last night. We had quite good atendance, just shy of three sets, with Maureen having to dance with a ghost partner at times. 

Reminder: Tomorrow, at 7:30 pm is the New Dancers' Celebration, at Knox Church Hall, 574 High Street, Lower Hutt. This is for all of us, new dancers, and not so new - the latter will be there to support the former ... New dancers, remember to bring your invitation (to get in at a reduced rate), and others, please bring a small plate of finger food. I heard that seven Tawa members attended Melva's preparatory classes on the last two Tuesdays, so I am sure everyone will have a great time! If you need a reminder, the programme and cribsheet are on our website. 


Last night's dances
  1. The Duke he was a Bonny Beau (R) - This was our walking warm-up dance. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/1783/ 
  2. Practise:   Ladies' Chain 
  3. The Paisley Weavers (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13727/ Starts with two chords, the one for the bow/curtsey, and one for the 2nd couple to cross to their opposite sides. On the NDC programme. 
  4. Kendall's Hornpipe (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/3435/ 
  5. Practise: Set to and turn corners
  6. Gramachie (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/2631/ 
  7. Jig to the Music (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13618/ 
  8. New dancers went home
  9. The Compleat Gardener (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14552/ 
  10. Peggy Dewar (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5204/ - A somewhat challenging dance, which is one of the dances that the Teacher's Certificate candidates may be asked to teach for their exam. Maureen took the chance to practise on us.


Coming up ...
Next Saturday, 17 October, 1-4 pm - Region Class - John Drewry Dances, St John's Church Hall, Bassett Street, Johnsonville. This class is aimed at advanced-level dancers - you can check out what is required, and register here. You can also just turn up and pay at the door ($15). The tutor, Damon Collin, invites people who wish to observe what an advanced class entails. His letter to clubs is attached. The region's class coordinator mentions that a gold coin donation would be welcome. 
John Drewry was a prolific deviser of dances - the SCD Database lists 793 of his dances! There is also a link to an article about him.  

Thursday 29 October - At around 8pm on our club night, we will have the club's AGM. Do please all come along, we need you. It won't take long and there will be a cuppa and biscuits. 
An AGM means the election of officers and committee members. We still need to fill some vacancies on our committee. Please refer to last week's email. 

Saturday 14 November, 7:30 pm - Carterton SCD Club Annual Dance, Carterton School Hall, 90 Holloway St, Carterton, (programme and crib attached). Carterton members will be having brunch on the Sunday, at a venue to be advised. (The original planned venue - Farriers Bar and Eatery - suffered a massive fire last week, and is not operational). All dancers and non-dancing partners are welcome to join them. RSVP by 7 November (cartertonscottishcountrydance@gmail.com). Make a weekend of it! Also happening on the same day as the dance is the Scarecrows' Big Day Out. Although not associated with the dance, it is a prominent event in the Wairarapa, and you may be interested in attending. As a fundraising fair for Gladstone School, it is held on the school grounds at 1471 Te Whiti Road on the 14th November, from 9:30am to 1pm.
