Tonight's dances
We had 24 people tonight - perfect for 4x 3C sets or 3x 4C sets. We had a visitor - Tracey, who, I think, is Debbie S's sister - and she did very well, considering this was her first time SCDing. Our dances tonight were from the programmes of Carterton's Annual Dance and our Final Night. 

  1. Will Starr (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14811/ This was our walking warm-up dance. Devised by Johnsonville's Rod Downey, to teach the Ladies' Chain in quick time. 
  2. Granville Market (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13621/ 
  3. Balmoral Strathspey (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/373/ This includes reels of four on the sides. (Our final night)
  4. The Caterpillar (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/8041/ This also has reels on the sides, but it is in quick time. 
  5. The Kissing Bridge (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14977/ Devised by Romaine Butterfield. It includes the "down the middle, change sides under the arm" sequence, which often gives rise to giggles and hand fumbles. It finishes with a poussette, which we practised first. (Carterton)
  6. Bill Clement MBE (J) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13577/ (Carterton)
  7. The Shores of Solway (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/6057/ We practised the 3C Knot before dancing this. Devised by Roy Goldring. (Our final night) 
  8. Best Set in the Hall (R) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12426/ Devised by NZer Helen Greenwood. Very popular, but includes some tricky moves. This is on our Final Night programme, but will be only for those who know it and are comfortable with it. Tonight too, it was danced by those who are already familiar with the "tricky bits".
