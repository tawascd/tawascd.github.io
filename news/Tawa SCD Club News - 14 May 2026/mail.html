Tawa SCD Club News - 14 May 2026

Hello everyone

As you can see, I am back from my holiday.  Many thanks to Denis for holding the fort and sending you the weekly newsletters while I was away. 

We had some lovely days in the South Island, a few days each in Arrowtown, Dunedin and Christchurch. The autumn colours in Arrowtown were fabulous. We had planned on going to the West Coast too, but the weather did not look promising, so we cut our losses and made a bee-line for home. 
 
The autumn colours on the hill behind where we were staying in Arrowtown. 

We had an excellent night's dancing tonight. Just on three sets, with Catherine dancing to complete a set. Great to see some people back whom we haven't seen for a while, and welcome back to Sean too. Catherine continues to prepare us for our Annual Dance with all except the first dance being on our programme. 

We had some step practice of Pas de Basque, which led into learning/practising the Poussette. Remember it's a square formation, and the "jingle" for the poussette goes: Away from the centre, quarter turn; up or down, quarter turn; into the centre, half turn round; dance back, dance back.  https://youtu.be/TEXJh0Sj-K0 

The last two dances were for the more experienced dancers. 


Tonight's dances
  1. Ecclefechan Feline (J) - This was our walking warm-up dance. https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/12848/  Ecclefechan is the name of a village in south-west Scotland. This dance is on Ngaio's Annual Dance programme.
  2. The Kissing Bridge (R ) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/14977/ We practised leading down and turning with the woman turning under the man's arm. The dance finishes with a Poussette. 
  3. Athens of the North (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/15643/ This dance includes Diagonal Rights and Lefts. 
  4. Mrs MacPherson of Inveran (R) -  This starts with Inverran Reels (the path of the 1s is an obloing "sausage" shape). https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/4784/ 
  5. The Yorkshire Rose (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/16761/ 
  6. The Gentleman (S) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/2437/ This dance finishes with an All Round Strathspey Poussette
  7. RoaringJelly (J ) - https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/5621/ The name refers to gelignite (read the Extra Info).
