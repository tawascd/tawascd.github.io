Tawa SCD Club news - 11 June 2026

Hello everyone

Only two more sleeps until our Annual Dance, on Saturday! (Just one if you read this on Friday morning) We would  love as many of our members as possible to come along and have a fabulous night! 
As usual, we will need everyone's help to make our special night a success. 

  • We will be setting up in the Ngaio Town Hall, at 4pm on Saturday. The committee will be doing this, and thank you to Ian and Debbie who volunteered to come along and help as well. We will have to set out chairs, put up tables for supper, put up programmes, and decorate the front of the stage. 
  • The dance starts at 7:30, but since we are the hosts, it will be good if our members can arrive a little early to welcome our guests - say 7:15. I will open up at 6:45, mainly for the musicians to set up. 
  • Please bring two plates of finger food - ideally one sweet and one savoury, to contribute to supper.
  • Financial Tawa members will pay just $5 at the door.
  • Please wear your name tags - I will bring the name tag hanger. 
  • Jo, Anne and Caroline will be organising supper, and they will aprreciate help from a few more members to set out supper tables, dish up fruit salad, and pour cups of tea and coffee. And to help wash up afterwards. 
  • Pack-up and tidy-up at the end - please lend a hand to put the chairs and tables away. If we all pitch in, it will be done in no time. 


Right, having got that out of the way, here's what happened tonight. 

We had a great night's dancing, Catherine packed a lot into the class - ten dances! We were delighted to welcome Linda and Ivan Roxburgh, who are on their annual trip to visit family in Waiouru, and always make a point of joining us for a class, and also our annual dance. 

We had three sets, which included several less experienced dancers, who were able to go home after the Notices, having done six of tonight's dances. We did the last four dances in 3C sets, with each couple only doing the dance once -  just so that we could pack in a couple more dances to run through as a last-minute practise.  

Tonight's dances
All except the first dance are on our Annual Dance programme, and the cribs are all on the cribsheet (attached), so I won't repeat them here. 
  1. It Should be Fun (J) - This was our walking warm-up dance. Catherine had written the crib on the board, so we could peruse and digest it before dancing. It is a 4C "once to the bottom" dance, so each couple gets to dance it only once.  https://my.strathspey.org/dd/dance/13465/
  2. Flight to Melbourne (R) 
  3. The Gentleman (S) 
  4. On the Quarterdeck (R) 
  5. Orpington Caledonians (R)
  6. Anna Holden's Strathspey (S)
  7. Roaring Jelly (J)
  8. Best Set in the Hall (J)
  9. John of Bon Accord (R) - This dance has two stars on the programme. Please only get up to dance this if you feel confident that you know it. 
  10. The Chequered Court (J)  - This also has two stars, so only dance if you know it.

Region classes - Advanced
The Region is running two Advanced classes in July.  They will be taught by Jeanette Watson. 
Saturdays 4 and 25 July, 1-4 pm, at St John's Church Hall, Bassett Rd, Johnsonville. There will be live music and afternoon tea.
For dancers who are already consistently competent in footwork and rhythm, and know most common formations without prompting. See details on the attached flyer.

Our own beginner classes
