Event Reviews

Dessert and Dance Night 2024

The big event in October was the Dessert and Dance night with dances themed around such sweet treats. With six sets on the floor Tawa members turned out in force forming four of them, including many of our new dancers who acquitted themselves with great aplomb. Ngaio, our 'sister' club closed for the night and we were able to welcome many Ngaio friends to Tawa. The dances were accompanied by Wild Heather, and their special arrangement of tunes for the dance Everything Stops for Tea, including Tea for Two and Tiptoe through the Tulips was a real treat.

Final Night 2026

Tawa SCD Club's Final Night for 2026 was a fantastic evening. With over 50 people attending, we had six sets of dancers on the floor. Tawa members made up more than three sets, and guests from Ngaio and other clubs made up another three. Music was provided by Rebecca, Lynne and Darryl. Catherine’s programme consisted of easy dances, all of which were walked, except for Best Set in the Hall, which was for "those who knew the dance well". Before supper, club president Denis thanked Catherine for her wonderful teaching during the year, and presented her with a gift of wine and chocolates. After a sumptuous supper, dancing resumed for a further three dances. Everyone went home feeling very satisfied, having had a great evening's dancing.
